About Me

I’m a Nairobi-based Photographer and Videographer specializing in events, portraits, documentaries, weddings, and creative film. With over five years of experience behind the lens, I combine artistry with storytelling — ensuring every project reflects emotion, culture, and connection.

My creative journey began in Kibera, where I discovered the power of visuals to inspire change. Since then, I’ve worked with organizations, brands, and individuals to produce compelling imagery that speaks louder than words.
